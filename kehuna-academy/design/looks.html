Bein HaArbayim

What the prototype wears now. Jerusalem stone in the day, dusk indigo at night, one gold accent, and the sky over the Old City as the living hero. Warm and atmospheric without a single cosmic cliché. The Hebrew is Frank Ruhl Libre, a real siddur face; the English is Cormorant Garamond.

Look two

Jerusalem Stone

No illustration at all. Pale limestone, near-black ink, hairline rules, and one copper accent. It reads like a beautifully set sefer, and it puts the Hebrew source first on every screen. The Hebrew is David Libre, the face of Israeli print; the English is Newsreader, an editorial serif with an italic that carries the questions.

Look three

Night Watch

Dark first. The Rambam says twenty-four posts kept the Mikdash every night, and this look lives there: near-black indigo, gold lines, the ring of twenty-four marks as the recurring emblem, one ember for the watch on duty. Cinzel gives the wordmark a carved, Roman-inscription weight; the reading text stays in Frank Ruhl Libre and Cormorant.

Look four

Techelet

The palette of the priestly garments: techelet blue, argaman and tolaat shani as a single woven stripe, gold thread, and white linen for the ground. The only look with a real color identity beyond gold and stone. Regal but calm, because the blue does the work and the reds appear only in the stripe and the on-duty mark. Noto Serif Hebrew and Cormorant Garamond.

Look five

Dawn

The modern one. Warm white, a soft dawn glow instead of an illustration, rounded cards with a little lift, and clean sans-serif type: Heebo for Hebrew, Manrope for English. One amber accent. It feels like a well-made meditation app, friendly on first open, and it is the easiest to read for a Kohen who has never opened a sefer.

How to choose

The structure stays the same in all five. What changes is who feels at home on first open. One and three are the most emotional and belong together as day and night. Two is the most learned. Four is the most Kohen. Five is the most welcoming.

Pick a number, or a mix, and I will apply it to the app the same day, with the site to match.
