# Mishmeret: the rebrand of Kehuna Academy and the app that follows it

Prepared for Rabbi Amichai Cohen, September 28, 2026. Working document, not final copy.

## What we found

Kehuna Academy lives on Kajabi (site 100722, kehunacademy.com, created March 2020, rebuilt November 2025).

- One product: the course "Rambam Beit Habechira," seven recorded audio chapters on Hilchot Beit HaBechira, chapter 8 empty, plus five Kajabi template placeholder lessons still published inside it.
- 24 members in four and a half years, all on the free offer. One five dollar gift. No purchases on the three paid tiers created in November 2025 ($36 monthly, $108 monthly, $1,800 annual).
- Several members appear to be Temple-interested Christians and Noahides rather than Kohanim. The current front door, "the laws of the Temple," attracts Temple enthusiasts broadly and does not speak to a Kohen about his own life.
- The Live Kabbalah Second Brain already holds Kehuna material: the conversation with Eli (Hod, Shalom, the Kohen who owns no land, May 2025) and the Three Altars / Mizbeach class (October 2020).

Decision taken with Ami: this is not a money project. No tiers at launch. One free door.

## The name

Recommended: **Mishmeret** (מִשְׁמֶרֶת).

From Bamidbar 18:7, "you and your sons with you shall keep (tishmeru) your priesthood," and from the language of the Mikdash, where the Kohanim "keep the mishmeret." It means the charge, the watch, the thing kept. It sounds soft in the mouth and it names what the app does: a daily keeping.

Alternatives considered:

- **Mishmar** (the watch itself). Strong and short, but in modern Israel it reads as security (Mishmar HaGvul). Kept as the name of the community feature inside the app.
- **Tamid** (constant, the daily offering). Beautiful for a daily practice, but heavily used by other organizations.
- **Kehuna** (drop "Academy"). Continuity, but "Kehuna" alone does not tell a Kohen the door is for him.

Domains, checked by DNS on September 28, 2026: mishmeret.app, mishmeret.org, mishmeret.com and mishmeret.co.il all resolve, so they are likely taken. mishmeret.co, mishmeret.io, mishmeretkehuna.com, kehuna.app and kehuna.co show no record and are worth checking at a registrar. The simplest path is to keep kehunacademy.com, which Ami already owns, and run the app at mishmeret.kehunacademy.com.

## Positioning

For the Kohanim of today. A daily keeping of the priesthood, with no Mikdash and no korbanot, built around the two moments the Torah already gives every day.

Tagline options:

1. Mishmeret. The daily watch of the Kohanim.
2. Mishmeret. You were given something to keep.
3. Mishmeret. For the Kohanim of today.

Voice: Rabbi Amichai's teaching voice. Warm, plain, sourced. No fluff, no cosmic language.

## Visual direction

Jerusalem at bein ha'arbayim. The palette is the sky at that hour over stone.

- Jerusalem stone, warm (#ECE4D4) as the daytime ground; dusk indigo (#141828) as the night ground.
- Gold (#A9802A light, #D6AE58 dark) for the one accent: the Hebrew letter markers, the on-duty state, the source references.
- Ember (#B45A36) used once, for the watch on duty.
- Type: Cormorant Garamond for English display, Frank Ruhl Libre for Hebrew and body. Both are on Google Fonts.
- The hero is the skyline silhouette under a sky that changes with the hour. Morning is pale gold into blue. Afternoon is ember into indigo. The sun sits lower in the afternoon.

Nothing cosmic, no sparkles, no purple gradients. Stone, sky, gold, and Hebrew letters.

## The app, reframed

Five destinations, each one native to the Kehuna rather than borrowed from a generic learning app.

| Destination | Hebrew | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Tamid | תמיד | Two moments a day, morning and bein ha'arbayim. One source, one short teaching, one question, one thing placed on the altar. Two minutes. |
| Duchan | דוכן | Birkat Kohanim word by word. Plain meaning, Rashi, and an inner kavana for each of the fifteen words. A "before you go up" checklist and a sixty-second kavana. |
| Mishmar | משמר | The 24 watches from Divrei HaYamim I 24. Every learner joins one. One watch is on duty each week (rotating by ISO week of the year) and hosts the discussion and receives the live class. |
| Beit Midrash | בית מדרש | Three paths. Mishmeret Kehuna, 30 days on the Kohen today. Rambam Hilchot Beit HaBechira, 8 chapters, 7 already recorded. The Inner Kehuna, 3 sessions from the conversation with Eli. |
| Ask | שאל | A study companion scoped to the Kehuna, grounded in the Rambam and Rabbi Amichai's teaching, always citing, never ruling on a personal shaila. |

### Why this order

A Kohen's daily life, not the Mikdash, is the front door. Birkat Kohanim, the cemetery, marriage, pidyon haben, and identity are the things only a Kohen needs. The Mikdash, vessels, and garments come inside the Beit Midrash for those who go deeper.

### Where the AI is, and where it is not

Generic AI chavruta apps already exist (Sefaria's Library Assistant, the OU app, Mishneh AI, Derekh, Hevruta AI). Ours is narrow on purpose: Kehuna only, sources named inline, halacha and inner teaching visibly distinguished, personal questions sent to a rav. The prototype's Ask screen carries this instruction set and answers live where the viewer allows it, with prepared answers as a fallback.

## Content debt (the real cost)

The software is the cheaper half. The writing is:

- 30 Tamid teachings for morning and 30 for afternoon (60 short pieces, about 90 words each). These can draw on Daily Tanya and Daily Zohar habits.
- 30 lessons of Mishmeret Kehuna (source, teaching, question). Day 1 is drafted in the prototype.
- 15 word kavanot for Birkat Kohanim. Drafted in the prototype, all marked for Ami's review.
- Chapter 8 of Beit HaBechira, still to record.
- 3 Inner Kehuna sessions, cut from the existing Eli conversation.

## Sequencing

1. **Now.** Clean the Rambam course on Kajabi (remove template lessons, finish chapter 8). Rewrite kehunacademy.com's front page as "for Kohanim" under the new name. Check domains.
2. **Weeks 1 to 6.** Personally invite 20 to 30 Kohanim, starting with Eli and the existing members, into one WhatsApp mishmar. Send the two daily Tamid messages there. This proves the daily habit before code.
3. **Then.** Build the working app from this prototype: sign-in, real mishmar assignment, the 30-day path, audio, and the study companion on Live Kabbalah's own Second Brain. Mobile web first, installable on the phone. Native apps only if usage earns them.
