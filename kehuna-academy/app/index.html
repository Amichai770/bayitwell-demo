Why two moments

In the Mikdash the whole day was held between two lambs. Nothing was offered before the morning Tamid, and nothing after the Tamid of the afternoon. The Rambam brings this in Hilchot Temidin uMusafin, chapter one. We have no lamb. The frame is still ours.

Mishmar מִשְׁמָר
The 24 watches

Every Kohen belongs to a watch

In the Mikdash the Kohanim served in twenty-four mishmarot, listed in Divrei HaYamim I, chapter 24. Each watch came up to Jerusalem for one week, twice a year. Here it works the same way. Tap a watch to make it yours. When your watch is on duty, your circle hosts the week's question and receives the live class.

About

Mishmeret is a daily watch of the priesthood, for the Kohanim of today. It is free, a passion project of Live Kabbalah, formerly Kehuna Academy. Every teaching here is written by Rabbi Amichai Cohen and reviewed by him. Halacha in the app explains the sources; it never rules on a personal case.
