| Routine | When (Israel time) | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Sunday content drafts | Sundays 6:47 | One Google Doc: 14 Tamid messages (7 mornings, 7 afternoons), the weekly mishmar question addressed to the watch on duty, and one search-driven article on a question Kohanim actually ask. |
| Thursday doors research | Thursdays 6:53 | One Google Doc: five new places where Kohanim already gather (never individuals), each with a public contact channel, a fit rating, and a drafted personal introduction from Rabbi Amichai. |

Principle for outreach: places, not people. No scraping, no surname lists, no cold messages to individuals.
