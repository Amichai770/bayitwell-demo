How it works

Your household teaches us how the home works

We begin with your routines, roles, languages, timing, religious boundaries, and exceptions. Bayitwell adapts to the household; the household does not have to adapt to generic software.

The right instruction reaches the right person

Each worker receives a clear daily instruction in her own language. Replies stay simple: done, missing, question, or unable. Routine confirmations remain quiet; genuine exceptions are routed for attention.

You see what needs you

Instead of watching every task, you receive one calm daily view: what is on track, what changed, and the few decisions that genuinely need you.

Principles

Religious boundaries are operational requirements

Shabbos awareness is not a calendar decoration. It determines whether and when the system may act.

Workers deserve clarity, not surveillance

Bayitwell assigns work by role, communicates respectfully, and shows presence and work state without rankings or performance scores.

Private household knowledge stays private

Every household is configured separately. Public demonstrations use invented data, never a family's routines or names.

Begin with one household and four weeks

We will learn your household's operating rhythm, configure up to twelve recurring routines, and refine the experience with you each week. The founding pilot is deliberately personal: we are proving what creates calm before making the service broadly available.

Questions households ask

Is this an employee-monitoring system?

No. Bayitwell is built to clarify work and route exceptions, not to score or rank people. It reports whether a household routine was handled, not a worker's performance percentage.

Do we need to change how our staff communicates?

The founding pilot is designed around the household's existing communication habits. We begin with what already works and reduce unnecessary complexity.

Is WhatsApp automation required?

No. The pilot can be operated as a private concierge service while the product learns the household's real needs.

Will our household appear in the demo?

Never. The public demonstration uses a fictional household and invented data.

Is this available to every household?

The first pilots are limited to staffed, Shabbos-observant households so that the service can remain personal and carefully supported.
