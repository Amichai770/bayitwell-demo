Behind the scenes

  • Four governed words. LISTO, FALTA, PREGUNTA, NO PUEDO are matched by rule, instantly. Anything else waits for a human, and a model outage degrades nothing that matters.
  • Fails dark. Religious boundaries are enforced by the engine, not by the operator remembering.
  • Worker dignity by design. People rows show presence and state. There are no scores, no percentages, no attendance file.
  • An append-only record. Every send, reply, and decision is appended, never edited. State is derived, auditable, calm.
  • Bilingual by design. The owner's English above the worker's Spanish, the house's own vocabulary preserved.
  • Channel-agnostic. It runs today over the channels your home already uses. No new app for your staff to learn.
